# Crypto World’s Fair submission requirements

Status: shared delivery checklist; use only current official Colosseum rules and FAQ as authority.

## Official sources

- [Colosseum Crypto World’s Fair hackathon page](https://colosseum.com/hackathon)
- [Colosseum announcement](https://blog.colosseum.com/expanding-the-arena/)

The official FAQ states that teams may begin before the hackathon, that judging focuses on work completed between the competition dates, and that pre-existing code must be disclosed. It also states that only one product submission is allowed per team and that the submission portal asks for product, team, repository, presentation, demo, and go-to-market information.

## Official boundary and disclosure

The event boundary used by this repository is `2026-09-14T13:00:00Z` (`06:00 PDT`). Registration on September 15 does not replace the official event start boundary. See `docs/WORLDSFAIR_PREEXISTING_WORK.md` for the factual Git disclosure.

C Market is one product shared with the CLOCK IN workstream. The World’s Fair submission should disclose that concurrent mobile work supported the same product and should not present a second product or duplicate team entry.

## Product narrative

Present C Market as a scalable Solana index platform with C3 as the initial basket:

- 40% Bitcoin exposure.
- 30% Ethereum exposure.
- 30% Solana exposure.

The narrative should cover index methodology, security architecture, analytics, scalability, business model, market insight, product execution, founder communication, and post-hackathon roadmap. C3 Mainnet settlement remains disabled; the current APK is a Devnet payment prototype and must be described as such.

## Submission checklist

- [ ] One accurate product submission through the official Colosseum portal.
- [ ] Public GitHub repository or approved review access.
- [ ] Product description, logo, integrated blockchains and tools.
- [ ] Two-to-three-minute presentation video.
- [ ] Product demo video of no more than three minutes.
- [ ] Go-to-market strategy, demand validation, distribution plan, and business model.
- [ ] Complete pre-existing-code and concurrent-development disclosure.
- [ ] Confirm current official deadlines and rules before submission.
