# CLOCK IN judging evidence matrix

The official announcement describes four judging areas. This matrix keeps the
claims tied to evidence that can be shown in the APK or the submission
package.

## Stickiness / Product-Market Fit — 25%

Evidence: Dashboard, C3 methodology, wallet balance view, Activity, and the
repeatable Buy entry point (`02-dashboard.png`, `03-c3-overview.png`,
`06-activity.png`).

Message: C Market turns a complex allocation idea into a small, repeatable
mobile flow with a saved local history of confirmed Devnet payments.

Truthful boundary: no claim is made that the user owns a C3 position or that
the current prototype settles assets.

## User Experience — 25%

Evidence: signed APK cold launch on Seeker, MWA wallet connection, clear
Devnet labeling, 5 USDC validation, localized UI, and the Buy disclosure
(`02-dashboard.png`, `05-buy-devnet.png`, `07-language-selector.png`).

Message: the critical wallet action is visible, understandable, and designed
for a physical Seeker rather than a desktop-only demo.

Truthful boundary: the final 5 USDC wallet approval is a supervised manual
action by Juan during recording, not an automated action by this package.

## Innovation — 25%

Evidence: C3 Core target methodology and mobile-native product framing
(`03-c3-overview.png`, `04-c3-details.png`, `PITCH_DECK_CONTENT.md`).

Message: the product combines a transparent fixed target with a wallet-aware
mobile interaction and a path toward a non-custodial Solana index platform.

Truthful boundary: the innovation is the product direction and verified
payment prototype; basket settlement remains planned work.

## Presentation / Demo — 25%

Evidence: `DEMO_SCRIPT.md`, `DEMO_SHOT_LIST.md`, clean screenshots, signed APK
identity, Devnet Explorer link, and `PITCH_DECK_CONTENT.md`.

Message: the story moves from user problem to physical Seeker proof, then
clearly separates what works today from what is planned next.

Truthful boundary: no fabricated Mainnet result, C3 holding, yield, or
settlement receipt should appear in the video or deck.

## Final judge-facing message

C Market is a working Seeker app with a real Devnet payment path and a clear
roadmap. It is deliberately honest about the current prototype boundary:
Devnet USDC payment is verified; C3 basket settlement is not yet enabled.
