# CLOCK IN pitch deck content

Eight slides, designed for a short judging presentation. Keep the visual
language aligned with the C Market app. Do not add unsourced market-size,
return, or performance claims.

## Slide 1 — C Market

Key message: A Seeker-first way to understand a diversified Solana-native
target.

Visual: C Market wordmark, Seeker screenshot, `On Devnet` badge.

Speaker note: “C Market turns a complex allocation idea into a mobile flow
that a user can understand and verify.”

## Slide 2 — The mobile problem

Key message: Allocation products are difficult to explain when the wallet
interaction and the product explanation live in separate places.

Visual: simple three-step path: understand → connect → verify.

Speaker note: “The user should not need a desktop dashboard to understand what
they are being asked to approve.”

## Slide 3 — C3 Core methodology

Key message: C3 Core starts with a fixed, transparent target: Bitcoin 40%,
Ethereum 30%, Solana 30%.

Visual: the three bars from `03-c3-overview.png`.

Speaker note: “These percentages are a methodology, not a statement about the
user's current holdings.”

## Slide 4 — Built for Seeker

Key message: MWA makes the wallet boundary visible and user-controlled.

Visual: wallet connection screen, Phantom selection, and signed APK identity.

Speaker note: “C Market does not handle seed phrases or private keys. Phantom
handles approval.”

## Slide 5 — A working Devnet proof

Key message: The current release completes a real 5 USDC Devnet payment flow.

Visual: Buy screen, app confirmation, Devnet Explorer signature.

Speaker note: “This is proof of the payment path. It is not proof that the
future basket has already been settled.”

## Slide 6 — Product experience and retention

Key message: A clear dashboard, localized copy, minimum validation, and local
Activity make the next interaction easier to repeat.

Visual: dashboard, language selector, and Activity.

Speaker note: “The user can return to the same product context without losing
the distinction between a target and a holding.”

## Slide 7 — What is next

Key message: The next reviewed phase is non-custodial C3 settlement and
user-authorized rebalancing, with explicit failure recovery.

Visual: roadmap: Devnet proof → reviewed settlement → separate Mainnet release.

Speaker note: “Mainnet is disabled in this release. The roadmap is not a claim
that those capabilities exist today.”

## Slide 8 — Why C Market now

Key message: C Market pairs a truthful working mobile proof with a focused
index-platform direction.

Visual: three proof points: signed Seeker APK, MWA Devnet payment, transparent
C3 methodology.

Speaker note: “We are submitting what works, showing what was verified, and
making the next security gate explicit.”
