## Project description

C Market is a mobile-first Solana product for people who want a clearer way to
understand a diversified digital-asset methodology. The C3 Core target is
Bitcoin 40%, Ethereum 30%, and Solana 30%. The current CLOCK IN release does
not claim those assets are held or settled: it presents the methodology and
records a user-authorized USDC payment to a C3 treasury on Solana Devnet.

The app runs as a signed Android APK on Seeker, connects through Phantom and
Mobile Wallet Adapter, displays wallet balances, validates a 5 USDC minimum,
shows a local Activity receipt after a confirmed Devnet payment, and supports
English, Spanish, Simplified Chinese, and Brazilian Portuguese. Mainnet is
disabled in this release.

## Why it belongs on Seeker

Seeker makes wallet-aware mobile software a first-class experience. C Market
uses MWA at the point where a user connects and approves a payment, while the
rest of the product remains understandable without a desktop dashboard. The
APK is signed, installable, and tested on a physical Seeker with Metro stopped.

## Honest limitations and roadmap

This prototype has not performed C3 basket settlement, does not represent
current BTC/ETH/SOL holdings, and does not promise investment performance or
returns. The next reviewed phase would define non-custodial settlement, quote
and route validation, receipts, and user-authorized rebalancing. Mainnet
enablement is a separate security and release decision, not a feature of this
submission build.
