## Feed, peg and independence policy

[Pyth defines unique pair feed IDs and Solana feed accounts](https://docs.pyth.network/price-feeds/core/price-feeds); the IDs in the candidate manifest came from the existing C Market registry and require fresh official feed-registry/account confirmation before production use. BTC/USD `e62df6c8…`, ETH/USD `ff61491a…`, SOL/USD `ef0d8b6f…`, and USDC/USD `eaa020c6…` are **reference candidates only**. None is a verified direct cbBTC/USD or Portal ETH/USD feed. Required proxy formulas are `cbBTC/USD = BTC/USD × cbBTC/BTC` and `Portal ETH/USD = ETH/USD × Portal ETH/ETH`. A custodial reserve assertion or bridge design alone is not a **live peg price**. If the approved direct asset/USD feed or independently reviewed peg market data is missing, halt valuation and settlement.

The [Pyth Core latest-update guide](https://docs.pyth.network/price-feeds/core/fetch-price-updates) shows server-side bearer authentication. A credential-free GET to the official Hermes latest-price endpoint on 2026-09-24 00:01:08 UTC returned **HTTP 401 unauthorized**; no price, confidence, exponent, publication time or evidence hash was obtained. `C3_PYTH_API_KEY` is an external, server-only future provisioning blocker, not a variable for Expo/mobile, fixtures or public configuration. [Pyth Solana push feed accounts](https://docs.pyth.network/price-feeds/core/push-feeds/solana) are a possible read-only channel, but an account for each required pair/shard, owner, layout, slot and 60-second freshness have **not** been authenticated here. No on-chain price has been claimed.

Secondary-source candidates must be independently operated and economically independent of Pyth. For each of BTC/USD, ETH/USD, SOL/USD, USDC/USD, cbBTC/BTC and Portal ETH/ETH, Security must approve an operator, exact account/market, liquidity floor at a 1 USDC pilot and larger sizes, 60-second update cadence, manipulation cost, TWAP window and outlier/divergence treatment. None is approved yet. Two RPCs reading the same Pyth account are only transport redundancy. A [Jupiter](https://dev.jup.ag/) quote may document executable market conditions, not independently establish NAV or a bridge peg. No unreviewed TWAP or guessed account is an acceptable fallback.

Candidate guardrails, all **disabled**: 60-second maximum age from publication to evaluation, no future publication or retrieval, 200 bps maximum confidence/price, 100 bps maximum primary-secondary divergence, exact feed ID and normalized integer mantissa/exponent, and independent source/operator proof. Missing, stale, excessive-confidence, divergent or unavailable inputs trigger NO-GO/circuit breaker; never reuse last known price to authorize a trade. At a 1 USDC pilot, stale feeds or dust can dominate economics. Provider identifiers in research fixtures are not authentication; a production verifier would need proof-backed on-chain account ownership or authenticated signed data plus governance-reviewed secondary independence.

## Snapshot and boundary

The strict `researchOnly: true` schema in `services/c3-mainnet/research/oracle-nav/candidate.ts` includes snapshot ID, policy version, cluster, retrieval/evaluation times, ordered asset price evidence, primary and secondary source URLs, feed IDs, mantissas, exponents, confidence, publication times, evidence hashes, peg pairs and a canonical SHA-256 hash. Exact fields, feed IDs, mints, decimals, timestamps and hash are checked; unknown fields including caller-provided `verified` are rejected. Deeply frozen snapshots are **fixtures only**; neither hashes nor caller-provided URLs prove authenticity. Current manifest deliberately records live feed observation fields as `null`; no fabricated prices or evidence hashes are published. This research directory is excluded from `tsconfig.build.json`, the package `files` list and the production service export boundary. No mobile import exists. A pre-existing dormant `src/oracle.ts` primitive accepts a caller-provided `authenticated` boolean and is not exported by `src/public.ts`; it is **not** an authorization mechanism and must not be connected to execution. A future production snapshot must bind authenticated provider/on-chain evidence inside a trusted server boundary, not accept caller verification claims.

The reference calculator is [documented separately](C3_ACCOUNTING_MODEL.md). It returns only `research_fixture_only` or rejection with reasons, never a live NAV/share price. Symmetry's actual share formula, fees, vault token balances, price accounting and USDC-only redemption remain unverified pending its official reply and independent Security/Squads review. Prior [public transaction evidence](C3_SYMMETRY_V3_TRANSACTION_EVIDENCE.md) is not a C3 40/30/30 vault.
