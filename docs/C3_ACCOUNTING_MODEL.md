# C Market candidate accounting model — not Symmetry's verified formula

This is a pure, research-only integer model for review. It cannot quote a live C3 NAV, mint shares, redeem, authorize a wallet action or establish the 40/30/30 basket on Mainnet. Inputs are hypothetical fixture balances, not an actual vault. The official Symmetry C3 share formula, withdrawal valuation and fee order have **not** been confirmed. Every transaction-bearing integration is prohibited until official evidence, Security review and Squads governance approval.

Internal denomination is `USD_E12 = 10^12` units per USD. Every monetary input is a canonical nonnegative decimal **integer string** or `bigint`; no JS floating-point input is accepted. A Pyth-style reference price is integer mantissa `p` and exponent `e`; `priceE12 = floor(p × 10^(e+12))` for `e ≥ -12`, otherwise `floor(p / 10^(-e-12))`. `-18 ≤ e ≤ 0`; overflow beyond u128, zero, negative and noncanonical mantissas fail. `floor(balanceBase × priceE12 / 10^tokenDecimals)` is each asset value. All multiplications are checked against u128 **before division**. Balances and share supply are bounded to u64.

For wrapped assets, candidate price `floor(referenceUSD_E12 × asset/reference_E12 / USD_E12)` requires two independently reviewed, fresh price pairs. The ratio is _not_ assumed to be one. BTC/USD alone cannot value cbBTC; ETH/USD alone cannot value Portal ETH. The production gate remains NO-GO without issuer/bridge peg evidence and source independence.

The fixture NAV uses `gross = USDC + cbBTC + PortalETH + WSOL` values and `liabilities = pendingDepositEscrow + pendingWithdrawalReserve + designatedReserves + accruedFees`; `net = gross - liabilities`. This assumes pending deposits are included in hypothetical counted balances but are **not yet share-owned**, pending withdrawals are reserved, and designated reserves/accrued fees belong outside existing shares. This treatment is a proposed policy, **not** a claim about Symmetry's actual account layout. Callers must provide every pending/reserve/fee field and an independently known expected share supply; omitted or mismatched fields fail. Dust remains in net NAV and benefits current holders until settled; explicit tracked dust is bounded to ≤0.01 USD_E12 and ≤net. Governance must decide ownership of higher dust before production.

Allocation percentages are `floor(assetValue × 10,000 / sum(cbBTC,PortalETH,WSOL))`, with the rounding remainder assigned to the SOL display bucket so the display sums to 10,000 bps. USDC cash is excluded from invested-weight denominator but included in gross NAV. Deviation is displayed bps minus 4000/3000/3000. Empty invested value yields zero/undefined economic allocation, not a compliant basket. The `sharePriceE12 = floor(net × 10^shareDecimals / shareSupplyBase)` when supply is nonzero; zero share supply with nonzero net, or positive supply with zero net, fails. This candidate value must not be shown as a live redeemable price.

Illustrative share formulas, all floor-rounded:

- Initial shares at hypothetical 1 USD/share: `floor(depositE12 × 10^shareDecimals / USD_E12)` only when both supply and net are zero. Zero-share dust deposits reject.
- Subsequent shares: `floor(depositE12 × existingShareSupplyBase / netBeforeDepositE12)`; requires nonzero net/supply. Pending deposit escrow must be excluded from predeposit NAV; otherwise issuance would be unfair.
- Proportional redemption gross value: `floor(redeemedShareBase × netBeforeRedemptionE12 / totalShareSupplyBase)`. It is **not** a USDC payment guarantee. The model does not estimate slippage, bounty, taxes or asynchronous conversion.

Flooring leaves residual value/dust in the vault; never round a payout up from others' assets. A redemption that rounds to zero is rejected by the candidate calculator. The exact owner and settlement of dust and fees need policy and Symmetry confirmation. No automatic fee deduction occurs in this research model. A 1 USDC pilot may be uneconomic after Solana fees, Symmetry bounties, token-account rent, swap spread and dust even if nominal shares can be represented; test size is a supervised candidate, not a public investment minimum.
